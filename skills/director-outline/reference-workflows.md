Ask only consequential gaps and offer that delegation, not an exhaustive questionnaire. Examples supply neither intent nor permission; delegated artistic details can remain open. Requested previews need intake first and separate user approval afterward. Unexpected issues pause only affected work.

Use selected models and parameters in setup and later creation without mandatory financial-budget, credit/balance or cheapest-option checks and without savings-driven downgrades. Honor user-supplied limits and report actual provider/account failures. Authorization for scope, overwrite, initial versus retry submissions, inflight and video remains separate.

## Undeveloped Serial Concept

Operation intent comes from the actual request: short/series includes needed new asset/sheet images after intake/reviews, without another generation approval, but stops before video. A later manual generate-video request is persisted as initial intent for its scope. Checking/monitoring only continues recorded grants or retrieves; neither a reference example nor generation intent grants unlimited retries or unrelated overwrites.

Front-load foreseeable consequential choices, not every artistic detail in these examples. Once initial needs are known or delegated, owners develop later details and Director continues authorized fixes and independent reviews without approval after each step. New blockers first use current materials/config/grants and professional judgment; only unresolved consequential choices, missing authority or a user checkpoint require interruption. Progress and internal review results alone do not require a decision packet or a final "start?".

A creation brief says a courier discovers that each delivery erases a memory. This gives baseline intent for exploration without a selected plot or unrelated technical settings. If plot and count are unspecified, Director develops three candidate stories by default, rather than asking who decides or offering three genre labels. Explicit counts override; supplied scripts, selected directions and prior scoped delegation are reused.

One candidate may follow a courier accepting memory loss to finish a dying recipient's delivery, only to forget why it mattered; another follows a search for erased family memories that forces a choice between recovery and a new relationship; a third follows resistance to the service, where exposing its cost requires delivering the evidence and losing the memory of an ally. These are craft seeds, not finished candidate packets: each needs developed motivation, conflict, causal progression and an ending/emotional landing suited to the actual brief.

Useful evidence includes the intended audience, ending, episode count and the user's initial common duration budget. A compact stage map may expose whether revelations change choices; one pilot scene may instead test voice and the premise's emotional credibility. Literary exploration can deepen a motivation that summary cannot settle. None requires every planning artifact. The tradeoff is early coherence versus premature commitment: a mature episodic engine may need only a pilot, while a tightly linked mystery benefits from tracking setups and payoffs. Requested preview materials still await actual user approval.

For real decisions, the author sends all foreseeable related questions together with explicit question boundaries, full explanations, stable labels, scoped delegation and conditional branches. All developed plot candidates remain ONE choice. Main retains the plan internally, splits only at authored boundaries and displays the complete current question/options before a native single-choice widget with one questions item. Related original replies and conditions return to the original author/context as a batch, not per question. Return early only for missing content/mappings, incompatibility or new decisions outside the plan; never infer provider conditions. No lossy rewrite or upfront plan dump. Host limits and fallback must be disclosed without hiding choices. Script/assets wait for their own relevant choices to be known or delegated; none of this establishes a fixed creation chain.

Provider configuration follows the same relay, one unresolved field at a time: provider -> model -> compatible ratio -> compatible resolution, with concrete choices and delegation for that field. Skip settled/inherited/delegated fields, avoid combined all-model menus, and do not preflight account access. User choice assumes entitlement for use; only actual authorized execution errors trigger reporting. No cost questionnaires or repeated approvals.

## Mature Screenplay Adoption

A supplied reconciliation scene already has precise dialogue and a deliberately open ending. The useful production question is what can be staged and what assets it actually uses, not which new concept to select. Scriptwriter can preserve the text while organizing scene timing and the script-owned inventory; Storyboarder can explore coverage, and Creator can compare existing visual identities with the scene's needs.

Evidence is the supplied text, preservation request, approved duration and available cards/images. A targeted staging adjustment may help an unfilmable action; a new novel or outline adds little if the intent is already clear. Adoption saves work without conferring acceptance: current independent script, shot and visual evidence remains necessary for full delivery.

## Continuation Without Restart

The previous episode ended with an ally refusing a delivery after learning its cost. A continuation can develop that consequence: who now cooperates, what the protagonist knows, and which promise remains unpaid. Relevant prior scenes, established asset states and any useful arc notes supply evidence; missing arc or novel files do not erase the established story.

A local episode sketch can test the new choice, while a longer stage revision may help if the revelation changes the series destination. Reopening concept selection would waste a settled premise unless the user actually wants redesign. The initial user episode-duration target stays common across the series, separately from inherited video settings; prior actual runtime is not a new budget. Appearance records capture this episode's facts without rewriting identities or previous episodes.

## Local Change: Source Or Derived

An envelope appears in the wrong hand in one sheet. Comparing script intent, shot direction, card and PNG distinguishes a shot-design contradiction from a card translation error or image-only miss. Those causes suggest different owners and repair scopes. Changing every layer would hide the cause and disturb unaffected work; fixing only pixels would leave a wrong source ready to reproduce the error.

For related changes to shots 3 and 4, stabilizing the actual changed group before assessing its outside direct dependents usually avoids repeatedly comparing temporary states. This is efficiency advice, not a gate requiring a whole batch to pass or a fixed owner order. A blocked member can remain unresolved while independent authorized work proceeds. A declared continuity link from shot 4 to 5 gives evidence for a focused comparison; numbering alone does not justify rebuilding all later sheets. Changed settings, references and actual output identities also matter. Unaffected work has an explicit preservation reason; suspected wider impact returns to Director for scope decisions.

Independent reviewers assess current materials in fresh contexts, including a separate aggregation context. Actual-success sets describe updated outputs, not all outstanding review targets. Neither the example nor a path authorizes force, paid replacement or resubmitting protected video jobs.

## Interrupted Production

After interruption, the script and shots exist, one sheet image is pending, and two existing images lack review. Current files, review scope/input hashes, pending IDs, receipts and video intents show what survived. The useful recovery may be same-job retrieval plus independent review of existing images, not a restart from concepts or the first missing optional document.

A missing script inventory can be reconstructed from its scenes without replacing dialogue. A changed card may need a compatibility decision even after its old job downloads. Retrieval uses recorded identity/settings and does not depend on new-generation approval; new or destructive work still needs current evidence and explicit authorization. Unknown inflight remains a reconciliation problem, not permission to retry. The tradeoff is recovering useful work promptly without confusing file existence, download success and artistic acceptance. Partial recovery can be complete within its commission while full episode readiness remains blocked.
